**250 words**

FastPress Labs is a small open-source studio for people who ship. The thesis is simple: a launch page should be useful before it is impressive, and a score you cannot inspect is not useful.

The flagship is **LaunchGrade**, a free, zero-dependency command-line tool that scans a public launch page in about a minute. It runs 53 checks across seven weighted categories: conversion, trust, technical, SEO, social sharing, accessibility and AI-agent readiness. The output is a letter grade, a prioritized fix list, terminal/Markdown/JSON/HTML reports and an SVG badge. It also ships as a GitHub Action, an MCP server for AI agents and an installable agent skill.

The public case study is YouLauncher. The first scan graded it **B- 71/100**. The rebuild fixed the trust, social and agent-readiness gaps, and the current scan grades **A+ 100/100** with one deliberate warning: no analytics. Both dated reports are public. That before/after is the product demo.

FastPress Labs also publishes **FastPress Start**, a zero-build launch page template with SEO, privacy, accessibility, a working form and an A+ public audit, and **YouLauncher**, a free one-page growth partner for people launching without a team.

Everything is Apache-2.0. There is no account, no tracking and no paid API in the core tools. The project is open to collaborations, audits, sponsorships and paid builds that fit the work. It does not publish invented users, stars, revenue, logos, testimonials or partnerships.
