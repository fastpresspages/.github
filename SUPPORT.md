# Support

## I need help with LaunchGrade

Open a [bug report](https://github.com/fastpresspages/launchgrade/issues/new?template=bug.yml) and include:

- the exact command or URL
- what you expected
- what happened (paste the terminal output, not a screenshot of it)
- Node version and operating system

For "why did this check fail?" questions, use a [check discussion](https://github.com/fastpresspages/launchgrade/issues/new?template=check.yml). For open-ended ideas, use [Discussions](https://github.com/fastpresspages/launchgrade/discussions).

## I want a human audit of my page

Use the free form at [fastpresspages.github.io/launchgrade/#audit](https://fastpresspages.github.io/launchgrade/#audit). Include the public URL and what you are launching. You do not need to buy anything.

The free audit is a short, human review, not a consulting engagement or a guaranteed conversion lift. If you need ongoing help, say so and we can scope a paid build separately.

## I want to collaborate, sponsor or hire

Email [meetcode99@gmail.com](mailto:meetcode99@gmail.com) with:

- who you are and what you ship
- the concrete outcome you want
- a link with public proof, if one exists

We do not publish partnerships, customer counts, revenue or endorsements that we cannot show. Proposals that need invented social proof are not a fit.

## I found a security issue

Do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Response expectations

This is a small, independently maintained project. Best effort, usually within a few days. There is no 24/7 support, SLA or paid support tier. Clear reports get faster answers.

## I want to contribute

Start with [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Good first issues are labeled in the repository.
